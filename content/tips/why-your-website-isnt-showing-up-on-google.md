<p>If your website isn't appearing on Google, you're not alone. Most small business websites have at least three or four fixable SEO issues holding them back — and most of them have nothing to do with complicated technical stuff.</p><h2>First: have you actually been indexed?</h2><p>Before anything else, search for your business name on Google. If your site doesn't appear at all, you might not be indexed yet. Go to Google Search Console and submit your sitemap.</p><h2>The most common culprits</h2><p><strong>No page titles or meta descriptions.</strong> Google uses these to understand what your page is about. If they're missing, you're invisible for any search that isn't your exact business name.</p><p><strong>Your headings are a mess.</strong> Every page should have one H1 (the main topic), followed by H2s and H3s. If you've got five H1s and no structure, Google doesn't know what the page is actually about.</p><p><strong>You're not targeting any keywords.</strong> Think about what your customers type into Google when they need you. "Web designer York" is a keyword. "Hannah's lovely website business" is not.</p><p><strong>Your site loads slowly.</strong> Google penalises slow sites, especially on mobile. Run your site through PageSpeed Insights and fix the big red items first.</p><h2>The quick wins</h2><p>Add a unique title and meta description to every page. Make sure your H1 includes your main keyword. Add your business to Google Business Profile. Get a few local citations (directories, professional bodies, local listings). That's it. Those five things alone will move the needle.</p>
